## Main takeaway

LENTERA gives us good prompt skeletons, not a reliability layer. It has **no retries, no output validation and no metrics code** in anything read. Its prompts assume a much larger model and an HTTP chat API. Our deterministic checks, retry policy and reporting must be our own, and those are the parts LENTERA cannot supply.
