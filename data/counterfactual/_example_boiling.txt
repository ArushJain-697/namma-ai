Meera and her father travelled by bus to a village high in the mountains. The air was thin and cold, and the road wound up past tall pine trees. When they arrived, Meera's aunt was already cooking rice for dinner. She filled a big pot with water and placed it over the fire. Meera watched the bubbles rise quickly. At the village, the water boiled at 90 degrees Celsius. Because the water boiled at a lower heat, the rice took much longer to soften. Her aunt explained that she always starts cooking early in the mountains. She added a little more water and covered the pot with a heavy lid to keep the steam inside. After a long wait, the rice was soft and warm at last. Meera ate two bowls and thanked her aunt. The next morning, they walked to the stream behind the house. The water was so cold that Meera could only dip her fingers in for a moment. Her father laughed and said that the mountains were full of surprises, and Meera agreed. She promised to write down everything she had learned about life in the village.
